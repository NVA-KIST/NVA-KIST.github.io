About Us

Welcome to NVA Lab — the Neural Vision Algorithm Lab (NVA-KIST) at the Korea Institute of Science and Technology (KIST) in Seoul. We explore the triad of AI, real-world deployment, and impact — combining deep learning, medical imaging (MRI/CT/PET), and robotic vision for healthcare and beyond — established September 2022.
